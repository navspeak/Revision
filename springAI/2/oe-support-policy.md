# OE Order & Customer Support Policy (Demo Document)

This is a fictional policy document created for demo purposes only, to
exercise the RAG tool. It is not a real company policy.

## 1. Order Cancellation

Customers may cancel an order at no charge any time before it reaches
"Shipped" status. Once an order is marked "Shipped", it cannot be
cancelled — the customer must use the Return process instead (see
Section 2). Orders in "Backordered" status may be cancelled without
penalty at any time.

## 2. Returns and Refunds

Returns are accepted within 30 days of the delivery date for unused,
resalable items in original packaging. Damaged or defective items may be
returned within 90 days of delivery, regardless of packaging condition.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business
days of the returned item passing warehouse inspection. Expedited
shipping fees are non-refundable except when the return is due to a
warehouse fulfillment error (wrong item shipped, damaged in transit).

## 3. Discount Authorization

Sales reps may apply a discount of up to 10% on any order without
approval. Discounts between 10% and 20% require Sales Manager
sign-off. Any discount above 20% requires Regional Director approval and
must be logged with a business justification.

## 4. Backorder Handling

If an item is out of stock, the order line is marked "Backordered" and
the customer is notified by email within 24 hours. Backordered lines are
automatically released for shipment as soon as inventory is replenished,
in the order the original orders were placed (first-in, first-shipped).
Customers may request substitution with an in-stock item of equal or
lesser value at no additional charge.

## 5. Escalation Procedure

Customer complaints that are not resolved within 2 business days by the
assigned sales rep should be escalated to the Salesrep's regional
manager. Complaints involving a potential safety issue with a shipped
item must be escalated immediately to the Quality team, regardless of
how long the original complaint has been open.

## 6. Employee Expense Approval (Internal)

Employee-submitted expenses related to customer entertainment or travel
require Department head approval for amounts up to $500, and Benefits/HR
sign-off for anything above $500 tied to a customer relationship.

## 7. Warehouse Transfer Policy

Inventory may be transferred between warehouses to fulfill an order only
if the destination warehouse's on-hand quantity would not drop below its
configured safety stock level after the transfer. Transfers that would
violate safety stock require Warehouse Manager approval.
