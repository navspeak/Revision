The following are the important types of probability: 

Marginal Probability: It is the probability of an event, independent of another event happening simultaneously.

Joint Probability: It is the probability of two or more events happening simultaneously.

Conditional Probability: It is the probability that an event occurs as a result of some other event that has already occurred.

====
 These are the three core probability types. Here's a concrete example to anchor all three together:
                                                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                     
  Scenario: 100 students, some play Cricket, some play Football.                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                          
  ┌──────────────────────┬────────────────┬───────────────────────┬───────┐
  │                      │ Plays Football │ Doesn't play Football │ Total │                                                                                                               
  ├──────────────────────┼────────────────┼───────────────────────┼───────┤
  │ Plays Cricket        │ 20             │ 30                    │ 50    │                                                                                                               
  ├──────────────────────┼────────────────┼───────────────────────┼───────┤
  │ Doesn't play Cricket │ 10             │ 40                    │ 50    │                                                                                                               
  ├──────────────────────┼────────────────┼───────────────────────┼───────┤
  │ Total                │ 30             │ 70                    │ 100   │                                                                                                               
  └──────────────────────┴────────────────┴───────────────────────┴───────┘                                                                                                               
   
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                     
  Marginal Probability — just one event, ignore the other:
  P(plays Cricket) = 50/100 = 0.5                                                                                                                                                         
  P(plays Football) = 30/100 = 0.3
                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Joint Probability — both events simultaneously:
  P(Cricket AND Football) = 20/100 = 0.2                                                                                                                                                  
                       
  Conditional Probability — one event given another already occurred:                                                                                                                     
  P(Cricket | Football) = P(Cricket AND Football) / P(Football)
                        = 0.2 / 0.3 = 0.667                                                                                                                                               
  "Of the students who play Football, 66.7% also play Cricket."                                                                                                                           
   
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                     
  Key insight — how they relate:
                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Joint = Marginal × Conditional
  P(A and B) = P(B) × P(A|B)                                                                                                                                                              
                       
  This is the foundation of Bayes' Theorem, which is next in your recall file.
