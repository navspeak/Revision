# Multivariable Calculus — Session Intro

## Welcome

Calculus is one of the most important branches of mathematics with extensive applications in data science and machine learning.

At its core, calculus is the mathematical study of **continuous change**. In the context of machine learning, calculus is heavily used in **optimisation techniques** — the workhorses of most machine learning models.

---

## Why It Matters in ML

- **Gradient Descent** — the primary optimisation algorithm in ML uses derivatives to minimise loss functions
- **Backpropagation** — neural networks use chain rule of calculus to update weights
- **Loss functions** — finding minima = finding best model parameters

---

## Topics Covered This Session

1. **Functions and Derivatives** — what a function is, what a derivative tells you
2. **Critical Points — Maxima and Minima** — finding peaks and valleys of a function
3. **Multivariable Functions** — functions with more than one input
4. **Partial and Total Derivatives** — differentiating with respect to one variable at a time
5. **Gradients** — the multivariable equivalent of a derivative

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this session you should be able to:

- Understand functions and their derivatives
- Perform differentiation of univariate functions
- Calculate critical points, maxima and minima of a univariate function
- Understand multivariate functions and their partial and total derivatives
